Overview

Overview

Dopamine is a luxury fragrance concept developed for the L’Oréal Brandstorm competition, exploring how scent can become an interactive, personalized experience.

Our team designed a NFC-enabled perfume paired with a mobile app that connects the physical product to digital storytelling, mood-based personalization, and community engagement.

Research

Research

Our starting point was a question, not a product: what makes an everyday beauty ritual feel emotionally rewarding? We looked at the neuroscience of dopamine — small, anticipated rewards that build habit loops — and used that as a lens for both the physical product (a 3-pod, mix-and-match scent system) and the digital layer (NFC scans that unlock progressive content).

We also grounded the concept in accessibility research early: the ADHD/neurodivergent angle wasn’t a late add-on, it shaped the core interaction model — grounded breathing, structured habit loops, and voice/high-contrast support were baked into the app from the first flow, not layered on after.

Pleasant fragrances can activate brain regions involved in reward and engage dopamine-dependent pathways, which may help explain why certain scents produce feelings of pleasure and encourage repeated exposure.

— Midroit et al., Current Biology (2021)

Key Findings

  1. 01 — Emotional fragrance already exists.

    Competitors such as The Nue Co., Edeniste, and Vyrao demonstrate an emerging intersection between fragrance, neuroscience, mood, and wellbeing.

  2. 02 — Smart fragrance already exists.

    ININUM demonstrates that app-controlled, refillable, pod-based fragrance customization is technologically and commercially plausible.

  3. 03 — But the experiences remain fragmented.

    Some products focus on emotion, others on fragrance customization, and digital wellness products focus on interactive rituals and engagement.

  4. 04 — This reveals Dopamine’s opportunity.

    The opportunity isn’t simply to create another mood-boosting fragrance. It’s to connect scent, emotional wellbeing, personalization, and digital interaction into one continuous ritual.

Design

The dopamine Experience

Dopamine begins with ‘the spark.’ A restless brilliance. A mind that moves faster than the world can hold. This is the opening of the story—curiosity, energy, anticipation. The phase represents hyperfocus and bursts of creativity.

  1. Step one

    Open your package to gain access to your new Dopamine perfume. Included is a keychain with an NFC built-in app experience, a pod backing for the dopamine fragrance, and the bottle. You will begin by placing the pod into the bottle.

  2. Step two

    You turn the nozzle to select which pod scent (#1 – #3) to activate, or blend all three as they are also meant to interplay together. Spray Dopamine Eau de Parfum, which activates the moment where scent meets emotion. Your spark has been unlocked.

  3. Step three

    Your ritual has now begun. Dopamine settles onto your skin and you are ready to take on whatever is ahead with confidence and a subtle spark that stays.

  4. Step four

    Once you spray, you tap your NFC keychain. The app responds instantly—tracking your ritual and starting your experience. Each scan will build momentum, mirroring sustained uplift, and evolving the cycle of reward.
